Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Reach us using the details at the bottom of the page.

How do I pay for my order?

We accept UPI. After you place an order you’ll see our UPI details (and a QR code where available). Your payment shows as “pending” until we verify it — then we pack and dispatch.

Do you charge for delivery?

Delivery is free on orders of ₹799 or more. Smaller orders have a flat ₹49 delivery charge, shown in your bag before you pay. See our shipping policy.

How does wholesale pricing work?

Each product lists a bulk price and minimum units. Select “Wholesale / bulk” at checkout — every item in your bag must meet its minimum for bulk pricing to apply.

How should I store spices?

Keep them in an airtight container, away from sunlight, heat and moisture. Use a dry spoon every time, and whole spices will hold their aroma longer than ground ones.

Can I use a coupon?

Yes — enter your code at checkout. It’s validated when you place the order and the discount appears on your confirmation.
