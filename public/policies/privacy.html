Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, city, PIN code, and (if you give it) your email address, business name and order note. We also keep the details of what you ordered and the payment status.

How we use it

  • To pack, deliver and support your order.
  • To contact you about payment, delivery or a problem with your order.
  • To keep basic business and tax records.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to deliver your order, such as your name, phone and address with our courier or delivery partner. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

We use one essential cookie to keep checkout secure (it protects against forged requests). Your bag is saved in your own browser so it is still there when you return. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Payments

You pay with your own UPI app. We never see or store your UPI PIN, card or bank details.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your personal details where the law allows.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.
