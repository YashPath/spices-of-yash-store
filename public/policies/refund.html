Store policies

Refund & return policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Food products

Spices are food products, so for hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns of opened packs or change-of-mind returns.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

  • Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with your order number and a photo of the pack and the outer box.
  • We will send a replacement or give a full refund for the affected item, at no cost to you.

Cancellations

You can cancel any time before your order is dispatched. If you have already paid, we refund the full amount.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the UPI account you paid from, usually within 5–7 working days after we approve them.

Orders we cancel

If we cancel your order (for example because an item is out of stock), we refund the full amount you paid, including any delivery charge.
