Shipping policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver across India to most PIN codes. If we can’t reach your PIN code, we will contact you and refund any payment in full.

Delivery charges

  • Free delivery on orders of ₹799 or more.
  • A flat delivery charge of ₹49 applies to smaller orders. It is shown in your bag and at checkout before you place the order.

When we dispatch

We pack and dispatch orders after your UPI payment is verified, usually within 1–2 working days. Delivery time depends on your location and the courier, typically 3–7 working days after dispatch.

Tracking

Once your order ships, we share the courier and tracking details with you on your phone number or email.

Wholesale orders

For large or bulk orders, delivery time and any extra freight charge are confirmed with you before dispatch.
